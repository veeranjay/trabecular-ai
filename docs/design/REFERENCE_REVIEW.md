# UI/UX Reference Review

Reviewed: 27 October 2026. Sources were opened directly and their current homepages inspected visually and structurally. This is a design interpretation, not a claim about their internal design systems or authenticated applications. The references are for UI/UX only.

## Canos — https://www.canos.ai/

Observed: a light homepage with a clear sans-serif headline, an asymmetric headline/explanation arrangement, generous margins and a large framed visual. Navigation and calls to action are comparatively restrained.

Adapt for Trabecular: give the anatomical object a strong visual frame; separate the primary task from its explanation; use a clear title scale and generous margins.

Do not carry over: veterinary/cancer language, dog imagery, clinical claims, the light color scheme or their branded image treatments. The requested Trabecular theme remains dark.

## Clean — https://www.tryclean.ai/

Observed: a warm dark foundation, a compact floating navigation container, editorial serif headings, a short action group and a large product preview with a quiet surrounding frame. The visible product preview separates navigation from the active task.

Adapt: use tonal separation, a lighter visual frame around the main work, short primary/secondary action groups and a simpler application shell.

Do not carry over: prospecting/chat product features or a giant marketing headline inside an engineering workspace. Editorial type is an optional brand influence, not a reason to use serif for numerical controls.

## Kairos Health — https://kairoshealthai.com/

Observed: a soft light/green composition, a rounded navigation capsule, a large italic editorial headline, generous whitespace and clearly separated calls to action. The page also presents features through a small tabbed selection.

Adapt: warm, approachable proportions; strong spacing around important actions; progressive disclosure through a focused selection rather than many simultaneous feature cards.

Do not carry over: forest imagery, floating decorative service cards, reception workflows or compliance statements. Translate the spatial calm into charcoal surfaces rather than copy its palette.

## OpenHack — https://openhack.com/

Observed: a near-black foundation, a sparse high-contrast header, a centered headline with a restrained warm accent and a prominent dark product preview. Technical information is organized into named product areas and evidence-focused demonstrations.

Adapt: dark surface discipline, clear focus on the product object and traceable findings that open supporting detail.

Do not carry over: security-console terminology, terminal decoration, threat-severity semantics or their gold branding. Engineering stress colors have their own physical meaning.

## Shared direction for Trabecular

The useful common ground is confident hierarchy, a dominant visual object, controlled accent use and breathing room. Their homepage whitespace is a composition reference, not a requirement to turn the application into a scrolling marketing page.

Our translation: a neutral black engineering workspace, warm ivory anatomy, readable sans-serif controls, restrained pale-lime actions, one contextual inspector and a compact expandable Deepgram dock. Borders and glass are used at actual layers, not around every datum.

## Current project observations

The current CSS and saved workspace preview were inspected. The existing theme and actual anatomy provide a good base. The next pass should address tiny secondary text/units, repeated outlined groups, competing navigation/context rows, simultaneous setup/results surfaces and the persistent assistant strip. The saved preview is historical evidence, not a claim of live application state on the review date.
