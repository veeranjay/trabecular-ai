# Product and platform

Approved direction: October 30, 2026. Trabecular is a reusable **SDF/immersed biomechanical analysis and closed-loop design platform**. CMF/mandibular fixation is the first showcase, not the company's limit. Accepted due-diligence corrections improve evidence and claims; they do not approve a manufacturer-only pivot.

## Three commercial pillars

1. Complex reconstruction evaluation for supported hardware/anatomy configurations, assumptions and geometric constraints.
2. Virtual-cohort evaluation of device/design families across explicitly identified anatomical and mechanical variation.
3. Closed-loop CAD remediation and VSP assistance: bounded geometry proposals based on computed evidence and an understandable review workflow. Deepgram remains the future voice provider.

## Expansion horizons retained

| Horizon | Intended users/work | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1: reconstruction and CAD/VSP partners | Planning labs, custom CMF/oncology/reconstruction and CAD integrations | Supported setup, anatomy/registration review, mechanical applicability and workflow value |
| 2: cohort device R&D | Manufacturers and engineering study teams | Lawful anatomy, sampling/coverage, uncertainty and qualified quantities |
| 3: orthodontics/dental appliances | Appliance and aligner workflows | Additional material/interface/PDL models; mechanics separated from treatment outcomes |
| 4: calibrated longitudinal prediction | Later research/clinical/OEM direction | Calibrated time/biology, held-out longitudinal evidence and specific regulatory review |

Geometry, compute, revision history, search orchestration and evidence contracts are reusable. New domains may require new equations, interfaces and validation. No zero-change/full-body qualification claim.

SDF/unfitted analysis and design loops have competitors/prior art. Demonstrate lower repeated preparation or better reviewability at a declared accuracy against capable automated alternatives. Numerical completion does not establish validity or clinical safety.

Moat candidates remain maintained context-specific validation, lawful anatomy/response data and useful design/physics histories. Each needs rights and measured value. Public libraries and two reference anatomies do not establish exclusivity. Paid demand/pricing remain hypotheses.

Current work is engineering exploration. Materials, perfect attachments, cortical proxies, loads and manually supplied keep-out paths are assumptions. No unsupported fatigue, healing, clinical sign-off, population-confidence or regulatory-compliance promise.
