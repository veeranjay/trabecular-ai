## Numerical routes

**Primary — SDF/immersed:** signed distances from actual component surfaces or declared parametric geometry, a geometry-independent finite-element grid, cut-domain integration, explicit material/body identities, loads/supports, interfaces, conditioning and thin-feature treatment. An immersed method still has a computational discretization; no universal crash-proof or mesh-free guarantee.

**Reference/checker — fitted tetrahedra:** preserve `jaw-exact-tet-v4`, archives and saved results. Its outstanding mesh/applicability gates remain visible. Never silently substitute it for the immersed method.

**Method checks:** known-answer static elasticity, independent assembly/comparison, measured convergence. A rectangular benchmark does not qualify anatomy or biological outcomes. Subcell integration, stabilization and approximation each need sensitivity/error evidence.

Shared geometry/parameter identities connect rendering, analysis and exports. Display interpolation is separate from raw assessment values. Synthetic cortex/tissues and manually supplied nerve paths are assumptions. Independent bodies must not be coupled accidentally through void stiffness or common unknowns.

## Credibility progression

Geometry → elementary mechanics → algebraic solution → integration/discretization → independent numerical comparison → measured comparator → applicability review. Calibration and held-out validation differ. Each horizon defines its own quantities and evidence.

Validate shape derivatives against independent perturbations before gradient claims. Bounded search is acceptable initially, with final fidelity/scenario confirmation. Studies report distinct anatomies, explicit assumptions and worst observed within declared coverage.
