# Project framing correction — October 29, 2026

The owner has called out that the review's proposed fixation-manufacturer workflow was being discussed as if it replaced the original project. **No such pivot was approved.** The review conflated a recommended initial commercial experiment with the overall company/product identity. This document corrects that interpretation; it does not erase technical or competitive findings.

## Original project baseline

Trabecular's intended product is a reusable SDF/immersed biomechanical analysis and closed-loop design platform, with CMF/mandibular fixation as the initial showcase. It was not specified as a single fixation design-change reporting tool.

The [detailed specification, section 1.3](../Project_Spec_detailed.md) defines three commercial pillars:

1. **Complex, out-of-envelope patient reconstruction:** biomechanical evaluation and screw/strut design improvement for difficult CMF, trauma, oncology and related custom reconstruction workflows.
2. **Virtual-cohort device-family evaluation:** repeated analysis across anatomical and parameter variation for medical-device R&D and supporting engineering evidence.
3. **Closed-loop CAD remediation and the VSP co-pilot:** use computed findings to propose geometry changes and support interactive review. Deepgram is the selected voice provider, replacing the original Bolna references.

The [master specification, section 6](../Project_Spec.md) describes the expansion sequence as:

- Complex reconstruction and CAD/VSP partners.
- Device-manufacturer virtual-cohort R&D.
- High-volume orthodontics and dental appliances.
- Longer-term calibrated remodeling and clinical outcome prediction.

The three pillars describe capabilities/commercial uses; the horizons describe staged expansion. They are related but are not the same list. The later remodeling horizon does not diminish the original multi-horizon vision.

## What the diligence should do

Evaluate and strengthen this original platform thesis. Identify customer access, competing capabilities, physical-model gaps, realistic validation requirements, defensibility, and evidence needed at each horizon. Distinguish the reusable geometry/compute/workflow foundation from additional domain physics and validation.

A narrower pilot can be proposed as a way to test the foundation. It does not automatically replace complex reconstruction, cohort evaluation, orthodontics, or the platform mission. A fixation-manufacturer design-change pilot remains an optional market-entry hypothesis, alongside the originally intended VSP/CAD/reconstruction entry point. The owner has not selected it as the new project direction.
