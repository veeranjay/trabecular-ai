**Access/review date for every entry: October 28, 2026.** Publication dates are given where identifiable; “undated/current” means the accessed page did not supply a reliable publication date. Search-engine crawl timestamps are not publication dates. Primary pages were opened and relevant underlying passages read unless a coverage limitation is explicitly stated.

“Contradicted” applies to the specified claim, not an entire company or project. Vendor documentation is primary evidence of what a vendor offers or represents, not independent validation of accuracy, customer adoption, or performance. Local evidence was inspected without rerunning it.

## Coverage and negative-search log

Searches focused on official Intact/nTop, Simq, Virtonomy, Ansys/Simpleware, Materialise, Meticuly, CustoMED, FDA, WIAS, Triangle-author, MeshLib, and dataset-publisher sources, plus original CutFEM research. Queries included implicit simulation/automation, virtual cohorts, implant testing, manufacturer FEA, intended use, modeling credibility, patient-matched design envelopes, licensing, and cloud pricing. The review supports correcting absolute competitor claims; it is not an exhaustive competitive census.

No evidence of Trabecular customers, revenue, incorporation, regulatory acceptance, private testing agreements, exclusive data rights, or patent protection was located **in the supplied materials and sources checked**. This does not establish universal absence. No authoritative registries were searched against an unidentified legal entity. A professional patent/FTO search, pricing quotes, procurement interviews, independently reproduced solver benchmarks, and a launch-country regulatory assessment remain outstanding.

Access limitations: a Simq/KLS Martin case-study PDF failed to fetch; it is not relied on as customer confirmation. A Materialise annual-report download exceeded the reader's size limit; no transaction-value conclusion relies on it. The YC library page opened without readable body text; this pack's startup recommendations are original analysis rather than an assertion of YC endorsement or a quotation from that page. The full paid ASME standard was not inspected; regulatory analysis relies on the FDA's accessible guidance and does not claim a standard-compliance audit.
