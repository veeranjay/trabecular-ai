# Demonstrable MVP

Revised October 30, 2026 after owner approval. This is a delivery target, not a claim that every gate is complete. Implementation and bounded checks are resumed.

## Required local workflow

Prepare → Design → Analyze → Compare → Evidence. Two distinct CT-derived jaws, explicit provenance/units/registrations, bounded fixation, immutable saved revisions, real calculations, replayable studies/searches and working exports. Preserve existing work/drafts. Unprepared imports remain inspectable and cannot silently acquire valid registrations.

SDF/immersed analysis is the primary development direction; tetrahedral analysis remains a named reference/checker. Display the actual numerical method and evidence state. Neither a beam nor tetrahedral result can masquerade as immersed jaw analysis.

## Buildathon: Implicit Lab

An interactive shape-to-physics demonstration with real distance queries, inspectable background-grid/cut-domain representation and bounded elastic calculations. Geometry changes affect both visuals and calculations. No decorative scanning animation presented as computation.

Method checks may start with known-answer thin-plate/fixture geometries before anatomy. They are method evidence, not a replacement mission or proof of jaw qualification. Immersed jaw completion requires actual assembly, meaningful interfaces/loads, field correspondence and numerical evidence.

Presentation mode reduces chrome and provides deliberate camera/section transitions through actual inputs/results. Historical/cached results are identified. No predetermined stress reduction or guaranteed passing candidate.

## Acceptance

- Geometry provenance, material/body separation, units, dimensions, supported features/gaps and rejection rules.
- Patch/energy/known-answer checks, true residual and force/moment balance; integration, thin-feature, grid-position and refinement evidence; appropriate independent comparison.
- Preserve and qualify the tetrahedral checker. Independent recovery is not physical validation. Current mesh/applicability gates remain open until supported by evidence.
- Genuine bounded search; reject invalid geometry; confirm the final candidate at accepted fidelity/scenarios; preserve infeasible, failed and unresolved outcomes.
- Studies retain anatomy/scenario counts, manifests and failures. The 2×3×3 target is 18 scenarios on two anatomies, not 18 patients. Report worst observed within coverage.
- Durable state, real controls, cancellation/recovery, stale-result invalidation, matching geometry/fields and direct engineering exports.
- Clean readable UI, keyboard operation, units/legends, shared comparison scales and reduced motion. Essential limitations stay available without repeated badges.
