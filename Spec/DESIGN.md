# Workspace design

October 30, 2026. Preserve black/charcoal and restrained pale lime. Hierarchy and spacing lead; glass, gradients and motion support them.

- A large anatomy canvas, one contextual inspector and one task navigation. Avoid duplicated names/status rows.
- One persistent result/model summary opens detailed evidence. Preserve numerical/applicability/freshness information, remove repeated explanatory badges from the default view.
- Slightly rounder surfaces: roughly 14–22 px cards and consistent control radii. Avoid nested outlined pills.
- Near-black backgrounds, subtle ambient gradients and glass on floating surfaces. Dense forms/tables get readable opaque backdrops.
- Smooth 150–250 ms interaction/panel transitions and deliberate camera movement. Respect reduced motion. No auto-spin or fictitious compute progress.
- Keep the primary action obvious. Diagnostics, provider setup, identities and long assumption lists live in disclosures/drawers.
- Presentation mode can reduce chrome and reveal geometry/fields, retaining concise provenance and meaningful failures.
- Real anatomy, sections, shared field scales and explicit deformation amplification. Scientific color maps belong to the model/legend.

The fuller [design principles](../DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md) and [reference review](../docs/design/REFERENCE_REVIEW.md) remain supporting material.
